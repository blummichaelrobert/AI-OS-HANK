## What this project is, and why that matters for the license

The AI OS is **primarily prose**. Its load-bearing artifacts are Markdown files that shape how a language model reasons — not compiled software:

- `pi.md`, `cos.md`, `meridian.md` — the boot sequence and Command Triad personas
- `theater-ops/_standards/` — the Function Contract, Mission Brief, and Battle Plan templates
- `validator.py` — the deterministic core beneath every structural check

**Open-source licenses were written for code.** Whether a language model reading these files produces a "derivative work" is legally unsettled. This NOTICE states the author's intent in plain English, because the license alone cannot.

## What is explicitly encouraged

**Build arms.** The AI OS is designed to be extended by others.

A Captain is a small, bounded unit of software authored against `theater-ops/_standards/captain_function_contract.md`. A Colonel is a unit of judgment authored against `colonel_mission_brief.md`. Any builder writing to those templates produces units that drop into any other builder's AI OS.

**That interoperability is the point.** The templates are the standard; the standard is what makes a community compound instead of fragment.

Fork it. Extend it. Share your arms.
